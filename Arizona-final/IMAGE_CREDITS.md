# Real clinical images included with Arizona

These are actual published medical images, not generated illustrations. The files are included locally so the gallery works without contacting the original image host. Keep their attribution and license links when publishing or adapting the app.

The downloaded images are unmodified. CSS scales them to fit the viewer. They depict separate cases and, for the CT pair, different axial levels; they are not spatially matched, the same patient, or a before/after treatment series. Source captions describe the examples; they do not establish an individual diagnosis. Preserve the share-alike license when adapting the licensed infarct image.
